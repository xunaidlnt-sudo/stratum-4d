Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Miqyas Labs ("we") collects through miqyaslabs.com and the Stratum 4D web demo, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, company, role, work email, phone (optional), messageYou send the contact formTo answer you and prepare a demo or quotation
Country of the connectionYou send the contact formTo route your enquiry
Visit statistics (pages, referrer, browser type)You visit the siteTo improve the site. Counted without cookies and without identifying you
Demo session dataYou use the web demoTo let you change the sample projects during your session
Technical logs (IP address, time, request)Any visitKept by our hosting provider for security and to run the service

The desktop app does not send your projects or personal data to us.

Where it is kept and for how long

  • Contact form enquiries: on our Cloudflare account, for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Demo session data: removed after a day without use.
  • Sign-in to the web demo is remembered in your browser's local storage until you sign out.

Who processes it

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the website, the demo and the contact form data. GitHub, Inc. hosts our source code and release builds. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop using it. Send your request with the contact form. We answer within 30 days.

Cookies

The website does not set cookies for tracking. The web demo stores your sign-in and your session in your browser so that the demo works.

Changes

We will publish any change to this policy on this page, with a new date at the top.
