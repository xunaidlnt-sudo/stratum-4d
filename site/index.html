P6 layout

Activities, WBS, EPS, resources, baselines, schedule (F9) and levelling (F10), laid out like P6 Professional. Reads and writes XER, and connects to P6 EPPM.

MS Project layout

The ribbon, an editable Gantt sheet, Task Usage, Team Planner, Network Diagram and the Task Information dialog. Opens and saves MS Project XML.

4D simulation

Load an IFC, glTF, OBJ or FBX model, link elements to activities by rule or by hand, split elements into pours, and compare planned with actual in 3D.

4D presentation

Viewpoints, section planes, an animation editor with camera and date keys, and video export up to 4K for reviews and client meetings.

Dashboards and reports

KPIs against a chosen baseline, the activities behind each figure, PDF and Excel reports by email, and export to an editable PowerPoint deck.

AI assistants

An MCP server lets Claude and other assistants open your schedules, check them, compare updates and record progress, on your own computer.

One tool instead of three

Most project teams plan in P6 or MS Project, build 4D in a separate tool and report in spreadsheets or BI. Stratum 4D covers the three on the same data.

For teams, Stratum Server runs in your data centre or private cloud, with sign-in roles and saved versions.

Direct to P6

The desktop app or your server connects to P6 EPPM or the P6 database directly, inside your network.

No lock-in

Projects go back out as P6 XER, MS Project XML, Excel or CSV at any time.
