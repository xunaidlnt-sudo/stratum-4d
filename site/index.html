Engineering Intelligence

Planning and 4D simulation for construction projects

Stratum 4D reads your Primavera P6 or Microsoft Project schedule, links it to the IFC model and plays the construction sequence day by day. Planners keep the screens they know; the client sees the work in 3D.

Try the demo Download

P6 layout

Activities, WBS, EPS, resources, baselines, schedule (F9) and levelling (F10), laid out like P6 Professional. Reads and writes XER, and connects to P6 EPPM.

MS Project layout

The ribbon, an editable Gantt sheet, Task Usage, Team Planner, Network Diagram and the Task Information dialog. Opens and saves MS Project XML.

4D simulation

Load an IFC model, link elements to activities by rule or by hand, then play, record video and check quantities installed by date.

Dashboards

KPIs against a chosen baseline, drill-down to the activities behind each figure, and export to an editable PowerPoint deck.
